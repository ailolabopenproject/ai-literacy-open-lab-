
# 4D AI Literacy Framework

## Overview

The 4D AI Literacy Framework is a practical framework designed to support responsible and effective collaboration between humans and AI systems.

Rather than focusing solely on how to use AI tools, the framework emphasizes how people make decisions, exercise judgment, and maintain responsibility throughout the AI-assisted workflow.

The framework consists of four dimensions:

- Delegation
- Description
- Discernment
- Diligence

Together, these dimensions support a continuous cycle of decision-making, reflection, and improvement.

---

# The Four Dimensions

## 1. Delegation

**What should be delegated to AI, and what should remain a human responsibility?**

Delegation involves determining the appropriate role of AI within a task.

Key questions include:

- What can AI do efficiently?
- What decisions require human judgment?
- What should never be delegated?

Delegation is not about surrendering control.

It is about consciously assigning responsibilities.

---

## 2. Description

**How can goals, context, constraints, and expectations be communicated effectively to AI?**

Description focuses on prompt design and communication.

Key questions include:

- Is the objective clearly defined?
- Has sufficient context been provided?
- Are constraints and requirements explicit?

Better descriptions generally produce better outcomes.

---

## 3. Discernment

**How should AI outputs be evaluated and verified?**

Discernment emphasizes critical thinking and verification.

Key questions include:

- Is the information accurate?
- Are sources reliable?
- Does the response align with the intended goal?
- Are important perspectives missing?

AI outputs should be examined rather than automatically accepted.

---

## 4. Diligence

**How can users remain accountable for AI-assisted decisions?**

Diligence focuses on responsibility, transparency, ethics, and reflection.

Key questions include:

- Can the decision be explained?
- Can the decision be justified?
- Who may be affected?
- Am I willing to accept responsibility for the outcome?

Diligence is not a restriction.

It is the foundation of responsible human agency.
